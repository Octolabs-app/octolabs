# OctoHome interface rework

Warm ivory, coral actions, sage/lilac department colours, original vector illustration, consistent icons. Chores gain date navigation, search, ownership and department filters, grouped due dates and editing. Meals gain a day picker. Groceries gain retailer links, source-aware prices and a Winners product lookup.

Product Design is confirmed installed but has no callable tools in this session. No tool-generated designs or human designer contributions are claimed. Notion working map: https://app.notion.com/p/3fbfaf1861a2813a9597d8eeef76e209

Price connector fetches only Winners public search listings, on user request, with an allowlisted target, timeout, response-size bound and short cache. Other retailers are website links. No checkout or cart automation. MUR prices cannot be mixed with another household denomination. APK remains offline; web lookup is available through the hosted app.
