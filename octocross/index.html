How to play

Fill each answer using its clue. Tap a square twice to switch direction. Use your keyboard, the answer field, or the letter keys below. Arrow keys move between squares; Enter moves to the next clue; Shift+Enter moves back. Tab leaves the grid. Previous clue and Next clue also move between words. Check word marks errors; Reveal letter fills one square and counts a hint. Clear word removes crossing letters too; Undo clear restores them until your next edit. You win when every letter is correct.

These are connected, free-form crosswords, rather than symmetrical newspaper grids. Daily editions reset at midnight UTC. Weekly themes rotate every Monday at midnight Mauritius time. Progress stays in this browser.
