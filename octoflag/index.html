Small flags. Big discoveries.

Pick one of four answers. Correct answers earn 100 points, with a streak bonus up to 50. There are 12 questions per trip. Keep exploring to earn XP and a passport medal.

World Tour mixes flags, capitals and geographic regions. Casual trips include a 50/50 lifeline: cross out two wrong answers when you need a little help. Two players? Pass the tablet after each answer.

The ranked Daily Sprint gives everyone the same 12 questions and 15 seconds per answer. The server checks answers, timing and scores. No lifelines in ranked play. Daily challenges reset at midnight UTC.

Joining the leaderboard is optional. Only your chosen nickname and score are public. The weekly board adds your best Daily Sprint score from each day of the last seven UTC days. Casual scores and preferences stay on this device.

Geography data uses 193 UN member states. Regions follow the dataset's geographic grouping. Capital questions omit countries with multiple capitals and selected disputed or changing cases.
